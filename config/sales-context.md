# Sales Context

This file stores the reusable sales operating context for the user. It is separate from the current company profile.

## User Role
- Apparel export sales / business development
- Primary work: custom apparel OEM / ODM sales

## Default Sales Motion
- Prospect → Research → Qualify → Outreach → Reply → Quote → Sample → Negotiate → Bulk Order → Reorder
- Primary channels: Email, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, Alibaba, WhatsApp

## Default Prospect Type
- Streetwear brands
- Independent fashion brands
- Emerging clothing brands
- Private-label apparel buyers
- Other buyers with recurring custom apparel needs

## Default Markets
- United States
- Europe
- Expand only when the user specifies another market

## Sales Principles
- Relevance before volume
- Evidence before assumptions
- Buying signal before aggressive prioritization
- One clear CTA per outreach
- Protect commercial margin
- Do not invent proof points
- Do not treat social activity alone as purchase intent

## Personalization Hierarchy
1. Recent product / collection
2. Product construction or decoration
3. Brand positioning
4. Recent launch / collaboration / expansion
5. Verified sourcing or production signal
6. Generic brand information only as a fallback

## Data Quality
Every material claim should be tagged internally as:
- Verified
- Reported by prospect
- Reasonable hypothesis
- Unknown

Do not present hypotheses or unknowns as facts.
