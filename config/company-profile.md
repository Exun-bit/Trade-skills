## Company Change Protocol
When the user provides a new company website or new company details:
1. Treat the new company as the active company only after the user confirms the change.
2. Research the supplied official website and extract relevant company information.
3. Ask only for essential missing details that cannot be verified.
4. Replace the old company-specific profile; do not merge old and new company data.
5. Re-check website, storefront, contact details, products, capabilities, MOQ, lead times, and claims before using them.
6. Keep all skills in the repository company-neutral.
7. Do not reuse the previous company's logo, contact information, customer cases, certifications, or commercial terms unless the user explicitly confirms they remain valid.
