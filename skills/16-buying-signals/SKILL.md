# Buying Signals

## Objective
Prioritize prospects based not only on ICP fit but also on timing and observable buying signals.

## Signal Categories
### Product Signals
- New collection
- New category
- Frequent drops
- Complex new garment development
- Expanded graphics / decoration
- New denim, outerwear, or cut-and-sew line

### Business Signals
- Brand expansion
- New retail channel
- New market
- Hiring related to product, sourcing, production, or operations
- Funding or investment when relevant and verified

### Supplier Signals
- Publicly changing manufacturing / sourcing arrangements
- Repeated supplier-related complaints, only when explicitly public
- New production requirements that suggest a need for additional capacity

### Timing Signals
- Seasonal collection development
- Upcoming launch
- Restock cycle
- Trade show / fashion event
- Product launch announcement

## Scoring
Score each signal: 0 = absent, 1 = weak / indirect, 2 = credible, 3 = strong / recent.

Separate:
- ICP Fit
- Buying Signal
- Urgency
- Contactability

Do not collapse these into one unexplained number.

## Rules
- Social activity alone is not proof of buying intent.
- Do not infer supplier dissatisfaction from normal brand growth.
- Prefer recent, specific signals with source dates.
- Expired signals should lose weight over time.
