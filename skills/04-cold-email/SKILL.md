# Apparel Cold Email and Follow-up

## Objective
Create relevant, concise outreach that earns a reply without sounding like bulk spam.

## First Email Workflow
1. Research the brand and select one specific, verifiable product or brand detail.
2. Choose one relevant manufacturing angle. Do not list every possible service.
3. State who YuMu is and why the message is relevant.
4. Ask one low-friction question.
5. Include the YuMu Alibaba storefront and WhatsApp when appropriate.
6. Write a clear subject line that is specific but not clickbait.

## Recommended Structure
- Subject: short and relevant to the brand or product category.
- Opening: mention a real product, collection, or design detail.
- Relevance: explain briefly how YuMu may support custom apparel development or production.
- Question: ask whether they are open to reviewing a potential manufacturing partner.
- Signature: YuMu, Alibaba storefront, WhatsApp.

## Follow-up Cadence
Use a restrained cadence unless the user specifies otherwise:
- Follow-up 1: around 4–6 business days after the first email; add a useful, relevant point.
- Follow-up 2: around 7–10 days later; ask a simple close-the-loop question.
- Stop if the recipient declines, unsubscribes, or asks not to be contacted.

## Reply Handling
Prepare concise replies for:
- Interested / asks for catalog
- Requests pricing or MOQ
- Requests samples
- Says they already have a supplier
- No current sourcing need
- Requests more company information
- No response after follow-ups

## Style
- Natural, direct, professional English.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, generic flattery, long company introductions, and pressure.
- Keep the first email brief, ideally around 80–130 words unless context requires more.
- Personalize the message to the brand's real product direction.
- Include only confirmed specifications and commercial terms.

## Safety and Sending
- Drafting is allowed; sending requires explicit user approval.
- Verify recipient addresses before sending.
- Do not send repeated identical messages or encourage deceptive subject lines.
- Respect opt-outs and applicable email marketing rules.
