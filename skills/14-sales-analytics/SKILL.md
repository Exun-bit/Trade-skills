# Sales Analytics

## Objective
Use measurable activity and conversion data to identify where the apparel sales process is failing and what to change next.

## Funnel
Track, where available:
- Prospects found
- Qualified leads
- Contacts found
- Outreach sent
- Replies
- Positive replies
- Qualified opportunities
- Samples
- Quotations
- Negotiations
- Orders
- Revenue

For Alibaba also track:
- Exposure
- Clicks
- CTR
- Inquiries
- Favorites
- Conversion rate
- Advertising spend
- Cost per inquiry

## Workflow
1. Establish the time period and data source.
2. Calculate basic conversion rates from available data.
3. Locate the largest drop-off.
4. Distinguish volume problems from quality problems.
5. Compare channels when enough data exists.
6. Identify the smallest number of actions likely to improve the bottleneck.
7. Set a measurable target and review period.

## Diagnostic Logic
- Low exposure → visibility / keyword / product coverage / advertising issue.
- Exposure but low clicks → title / main image / product relevance issue.
- Clicks but few inquiries → product page / trust / offer / CTA issue.
- Many inquiries but few quotations → qualification or response issue.
- Quotations but few samples → pricing / trust / product fit / follow-up issue.
- Samples but few bulk orders → sample quality / commercial terms / follow-up / buyer timing issue.

These are diagnostic hypotheses, not automatic conclusions.

## Rules
- Do not claim causation from one metric.
- Use percentages and absolute counts together.
- Mark small sample sizes as unreliable.
- Do not compare periods with materially different traffic sources without accounting for the difference.
- Recommend actions that can be measured afterward.
