# Pipeline Review

## Objective
Convert a lead sheet into a prioritized work queue.

## Workflow
1. Read the current lead table or CRM export.
2. Group records by stage.
3. Identify follow-ups due, overdue leads, hot opportunities, stalled opportunities, missing contact data, and opportunities without a defined next action.
4. Assess pipeline concentration and risk.
5. Select the highest-value actions for the next working period.
6. Recommend what to stop doing if it produces little evidence of return.

## Output
- Pipeline snapshot
- Top 5 actions
- Follow-ups due
- At-risk opportunities
- Research backlog
- Dormant reactivation list
- Metrics to review next time

## Rules
- Do not equate the number of leads with pipeline health.
- Do not count unqualified prospects as real opportunities.
- Use the latest known interaction date.
