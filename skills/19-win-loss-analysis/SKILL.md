# Win-Loss Analysis

## Objective
Turn sales outcomes into reusable learning rather than treating every lost deal as an isolated event.

## Analyze
- Lead source
- Product category
- Buyer type
- Lead grade
- Buying signal
- Contact quality
- Response speed
- Quoted price position
- MOQ
- Sample result
- Quality concerns
- Lead time
- Existing supplier
- Competitor presence
- Buyer timing
- Final outcome
- Stated reason
- Inferred reason, clearly labeled as hypothesis

## Patterns
Group outcomes by product, market, customer type, lead source, price band, MOQ, sample, response time, objection, and competitor.

## Output
- Win patterns
- Loss patterns
- Stalled-deal patterns
- Evidence strength
- Most likely causes
- Changes to ICP
- Changes to outreach
- Changes to quotation
- Changes to product positioning

## Rules
- A buyer's stated reason is evidence, not necessarily the complete cause.
- Do not turn a small sample into a broad conclusion.
- Separate observed facts from interpretation.
