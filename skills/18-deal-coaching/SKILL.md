# Deal Coaching

## Objective
Help the user decide what to do next on an active apparel opportunity instead of simply writing another follow-up.

## Workflow
1. Reconstruct the deal timeline from the available conversation.
2. Identify buyer objective, product, quantity, price position, sample status, approval status, decision-maker status, competitor / existing supplier status, and timing.
3. Score deal health across product fit, commercial fit, relationship, buyer engagement, decision clarity, and timing.
4. Identify the biggest current risk.
5. Recommend one next action.
6. Draft the buyer-facing message if needed.

## Risk Flags
- Buyer requests repeated price reductions
- No clear decision-maker
- Sample not approved
- Long unexplained silence
- Existing supplier entrenched
- Unconfirmed requirements
- Commercial terms not aligned
- Buyer urgency without internal confirmation

## Output
Deal status → Evidence → Main risk → Recommended action → Buyer message → Follow-up timing.

## Rules
- Do not confuse friendliness with buying intent.
- Do not declare a deal lost solely because the buyer is slow to reply.
- Do not recommend discounts without a commercial reason.
