# Lead Management

## Objective
Turn prospecting activity into a measurable pipeline instead of a collection of disconnected contacts.

## Lead Record
Track:
Brand | Website | Country | Contact | Role | Source | Product Fit | Buying Signal | Lead Grade | Last Contact | Current Stage | Next Action | Follow-up Date | Notes

## Lead Stages
1. New
2. Researched
3. Contacted
4. Replied
5. Qualified
6. Sample / quotation
7. Negotiation
8. Order
9. Dormant
10. Lost

## Scoring
Evaluate:
- Product fit
- Brand activity
- Custom-manufacturing relevance
- Contact quality
- Buying signals
- Response level
- Commercial potential
- Relationship strength

Use A/B/C as a simple priority label, but keep the evidence behind the score.

## Workflow
1. Deduplicate the lead.
2. Update the latest interaction.
3. Record the buyer's actual position rather than guessing intent.
4. Define one next action.
5. Assign a realistic follow-up date.
6. Escalate or reduce priority based on new evidence.
7. Periodically identify dormant leads worth reactivation.

## Reactivation
Use a genuine reason to reconnect:
- New product capability
- Relevant sample / product idea
- Seasonal collection timing
- New quotation opportunity
- Previously discussed product update

Do not invent a reason just to create urgency.

## Output
When asked to organize leads, provide a spreadsheet-friendly table and identify:
- Hot leads
- Leads needing research
- Follow-ups due
- Dormant leads
- Leads with missing decision-maker information
