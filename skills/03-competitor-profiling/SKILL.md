# Apparel Competitor Profiling

## Objective
Understand how competing apparel suppliers present their services and identify credible differentiation opportunities for the user's current company or represented supplier.

## Workflow
1. Define the competitor set and comparison scope: product category, target market, OEM/ODM model, or buyer segment.
2. Review official websites, B2B storefronts, product catalogs, public social accounts, and credible third-party sources.
3. Read `config/company-profile.md` to understand the current supplier's verified scope. If it is incomplete, mark comparisons as unknown rather than filling gaps.
4. Record only published or otherwise verifiable information.
5. Compare product focus, customization scope, fabrics, decoration methods, development support, sample process, MOQ, lead times, quality controls, certifications, markets, and case studies when disclosed.
6. Note how competitors communicate: positioning, proof points, imagery, calls to action, and response channels.
7. Identify gaps or opportunities for the current supplier, but do not present them as capabilities until confirmed.
8. Recommend evidence-backed positioning and content improvements.

## Analysis
Separate:
- Verified competitor facts
- Competitor marketing claims not independently verified
- Missing information
- Strategic opportunities for the current supplier

## Rules
- Never invent competitor specifications or quote unpublished terms as facts.
- Do not assume a supplier's certifications are current; verify validity and scope where possible.
- Do not use competitor logos, customer logos, or protected artwork in marketing assets without permission.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as “better quality”, “lower price”, or “faster delivery”.
